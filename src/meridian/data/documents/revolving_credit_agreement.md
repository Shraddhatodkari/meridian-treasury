## RCF-8.1 Voluntary prepayment
The Borrower may prepay any loan on three business days' prior notice, in a minimum amount of
USD 5,000,000 and in integral multiples of USD 1,000,000, without premium or penalty.

## RCF-9.1 Interest
Loans bear interest at SOFR plus the Applicable Margin of 2.50% per annum, reset monthly and payable
monthly in arrears. (Meridian prices the facility with the published overnight SOFR as a proxy for
Term SOFR, which requires a CME licence.)

## RCF-22.1 Minimum liquidity covenant
The Borrower shall ensure that Liquidity (unrestricted cash of the Group plus undrawn commitments) is
not less than USD 250,000,000 at all times.

## RCF-22.2 Net leverage covenant
The Borrower shall ensure that the ratio of Net Debt to LTM EBITDA does not exceed 3.00:1 on each
quarterly test date.

## RCF-22.3 Interest cover covenant
The Borrower shall ensure that the ratio of LTM EBITDA to Net Interest Expense is not less than 4.00:1
on each quarterly test date.

## RCF-23.4 Restricted cash
Cash held in jurisdictions where repatriation is restricted by law shall be counted as Liquidity only
to the extent it is available to meet obligations of the entity holding it.
