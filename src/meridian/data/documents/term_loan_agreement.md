## TLA-5.1 Interest
Loans bear interest at SOFR plus a margin of 1.75% per annum, payable monthly in arrears.

## TLA-6.1 Voluntary prepayment
The Borrower may prepay the loan, in whole or in part, on five business days' notice in a minimum amount
of USD 10,000,000 and integral multiples of USD 5,000,000, without premium. Prepaid amounts may not be
re-borrowed.

## TLA-7.1 Scheduled amortisation
The Borrower shall repay 5% of the original principal per annum in equal quarterly instalments on the
last business day of each calendar quarter.

## TLA-9.1 Final maturity
All outstanding amounts are due on 30 September 2027. The Borrower should mandate refinancing banks no
later than the date required by its debt and funding policy.
