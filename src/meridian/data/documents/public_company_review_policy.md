# Meridian Reference Policy — Listed-Company Liquidity & Working-Capital Review (illustrative)
<!-- meta: version=2026.1; effective=2026-10-01; owner=Meridian Governance (reference framework); classification=REFERENCE -->

This is Meridian's reference governance framework for analysing a listed company from public filings. It is NOT the internal treasury policy of the company being analysed (for example Caterpillar Inc.). A client deploying Meridian replaces these thresholds with its own approved policy.

## PC-1.1 Liquidity coverage of short-term debt
Liquidity (cash and cash equivalents plus undrawn committed credit facilities) should cover debt due within twelve months at least 1.25 times. Below that, a liquidity policy review is proposed to the CFO.

## PC-2.1 Refinancing readiness
When debt due within twelve months exceeds 25% of liquidity, a refinancing plan covering issuance windows, funding mix and pre-funding is proposed to the CFO. The plan is a decision mandate and creates no payment.

## PC-3.1 Working-capital deterioration
When DSO or DIO rises by five days or more year on year (trailing-twelve-month denominators), a working-capital review is proposed to the Group Treasurer and the Working Capital Manager. The quantified cash effect must be labelled ESTIMATED and must not be presented as a guaranteed saving.

## PC-3.2 Peer comparisons
Peer benchmarks from public filings are context only. A recommendation must not rely solely on the gap to a peer median when business models differ (for example captive finance, dealer receivables, long-cycle inventory).

## PC-4.1 Shareholder returns versus free cash flow
When trailing-twelve-month shareholder returns (dividends plus repurchases) exceed trailing-twelve-month free cash flow, a capital-allocation review is proposed to the CFO.

## PC-5.1 Repricing exposure
When the annual cost of a 100 basis-point rise on debt repricing or maturing within twelve months exceeds 0.5% of EBITDA, a rate-risk review is proposed to the Group Treasurer. Where the fixed/floating mix and hedge positions are not available from public data, the review must state that limitation.

## PC-6.1 Advisory nature
Recommendations derived from public filings are decision mandates for management. They never move money. Approval follows the approval matrix; segregation of duties applies; issuing an approved mandate requires a second authorised person (dual control).

## PC-6.2 Disclosure of data limitations
Every recommendation must show its sources, filing periods, calculations, assumptions and the data that is NOT AVAILABLE FROM PUBLIC DATA.
