# Cash Repatriation Guidelines by Jurisdiction (FICTIONAL, ILLUSTRATIVE — confirm with Tax)
<!-- meta: version=2026.1; effective=2026-01-01; owner=Tax / Treasury; classification=FICTIONAL (evaluation) -->

## CR-1 China
Cash may leave the PRC entity only through declared dividends after statutory audit, or approved
intercompany lending quotas. No discretionary upstreaming is available in the current quarter.

## CR-2 India
Upstreaming is limited to USD 3 million per quarter through approved channels. Withholding and
transaction taxes of approximately 50 basis points apply.

## CR-3 Brazil
Cross-border transfers incur IOF tax of approximately 0.38%. Up to USD 6 million may be upstreamed in
the current quarter via intercompany loan repayment.

## CR-4 South Africa
Exchange control approval permits up to USD 2 million per quarter. Transfers above this require prior
central-bank approval.

## CR-5 Other jurisdictions
For entities without capital controls, cash above the operating buffer may be swept to Group Treasury,
subject to foreign-exchange spreads and transfer costs.
