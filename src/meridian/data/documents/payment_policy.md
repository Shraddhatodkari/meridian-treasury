# Orion Industrial Group — Payment & Supplier Terms Policy (FICTIONAL)
<!-- meta: version=2026.1; effective=2026-01-15; owner=Group Treasury; classification=FICTIONAL (evaluation) -->

## PP-1.1 Pay on terms
Supplier invoices are paid on their agreed due date. Paying earlier than due without capturing an
available early-payment discount is not permitted, because it consumes liquidity for no return.

## PP-1.2 Early-payment discounts
Available discounts must be captured where the implied annual return exceeds the Group's marginal
cost of funds and liquidity allows (see TP-2.3). Missed discounts are reported monthly.

## PP-2.1 Payment formats
Payments are prepared as ISO 20022 pain.001 files and validated (IBAN check digits, currency, amount,
duplicate detection, cut-off) before release under TP-7.2.
