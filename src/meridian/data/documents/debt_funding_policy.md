# Orion Industrial Group — Debt & Funding Policy (FICTIONAL)
<!-- meta: version=2026.1; effective=2026-02-01; owner=CFO; classification=FICTIONAL (evaluation) -->

## DP-1.1 Objectives
Maintain committed funding sufficient for 18 months of forecast needs, a balanced maturity profile and
an interest-rate mix that limits earnings volatility. The CFO owns this policy; the Board approves new
long-term debt above USD 250 million.

## DP-2.1 Refinancing horizon
Refinancing of any facility must be launched (banks mandated) no later than 18 months before its final
maturity. A refinancing plan requires CFO approval; the cost estimate must state its market inputs.

## DP-3.1 Fixed and floating mix
Between 40% and 75% of gross debt should bear fixed rates. Outside this band Treasury must propose a
remediation (swap, new issue or prepayment of floating debt) to the CFO.

## DP-4.1 Maturity concentration
No more than 40% of gross debt may mature in any rolling 12-month period.
