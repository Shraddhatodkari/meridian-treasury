# Product brief: prompt to prototype

**Users and jobs-to-be-done**

| User | Needs to |
|---|---|
| Group Treasurer | Know where cash is and will be, move it efficiently, stay within covenants |
| CFO | Get decisions with the numbers and risks in one page, and approve big moves |
| AR / treasury analyst | Prioritise collections, prepare the weekly forecast in minutes instead of days |

**MVP scope (built):**
- 13-week probabilistic forecast
- Collections prioritisation
- Early-pay discounts
- Liquidity optimiser
- NL scenarios
- Policy Q&A
- Approvals with payment drafts
- Audit trail

**Out of scope (next):**
- FX hedging recommendations
- Bank API connectivity (camt.053 statement ingestion)
- In-house bank netting
- Supplier-finance programmes

**Success metrics:**
- Forecast WAPE vs current process
- Hours per weekly forecast cycle
- Average revolver balance
- Idle cash above buffers
- Recommendation acceptance rate
- Zero control exceptions
